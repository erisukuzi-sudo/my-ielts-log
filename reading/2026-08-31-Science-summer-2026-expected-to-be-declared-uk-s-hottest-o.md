# Summer 2026 expected to be declared UK's hottest on record

**Category:** Science

[Original Article](https://www.bbc.co.uk/weather/articles/c99dypl3jgmo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

Met Office figures due on Tuesday are likely to show that the previous record, set in 2025, has been broken after an unprecedented summer of persistent heat.
