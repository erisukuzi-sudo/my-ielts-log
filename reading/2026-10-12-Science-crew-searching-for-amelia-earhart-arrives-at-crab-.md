# Crew searching for Amelia Earhart arrives at crab-infested island

**Category:** Science

[Original Article](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6pvgryp8yv0o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

Researchers will sleep on their ship to avoid giant crabs that can lift heavy weights, split open coconuts, and may have played a role in Earhart's demise.
