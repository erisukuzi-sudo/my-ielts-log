**Category:** Education

[Original Article](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6ly3027820lo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

Ofcom says photo and digital ID, banking checks or facial age estimation can help prove someone is 16 or over.
