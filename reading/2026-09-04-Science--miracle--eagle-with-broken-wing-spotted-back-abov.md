# 'Miracle' eagle with broken wing spotted back above Scottish coast

**Category:** Science

[Original Article](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crerw2nj81zo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

A sea eagle that confounded experts by surviving with a broken wing has been spotted back above Scotland's west coast.
