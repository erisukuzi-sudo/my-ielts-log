# UK to get best eclipse since 1999 as Moon blocks out Sun across Europe

**Category:** Science

[Original Article](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2jr1ew2v1o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

Up to 96% of the Sun will be covered by the Moon in the UK, while parts of Spain, Greenland, Iceland, Portugal and Russia will see a total eclipse.
