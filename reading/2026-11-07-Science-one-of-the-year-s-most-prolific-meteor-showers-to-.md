# One of the year's most prolific meteor showers to light up the night sky this weekend

**Category:** Science

[Original Article](https://www.bbc.co.uk/weather/articles/cmlykqq7zg9zo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

The Leonid meteor shower, known for its bright and fast meteors, will kick off this weekend and continue until the end of November.
